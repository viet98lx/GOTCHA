## 🔊 Human Presence Detection Using Active Acoustic Sensing

We developed a prototype **smart speaker** that detects human presence in a room using **active acoustic sensing**. The device emits short **chirp signals**, records the reflections, and analyzes the acoustic changes to determine whether a person is present.

- 📡 **How it works**: Chirp sound is played and captured via microphone; the echo patterns differ depending on the presence of a human body.
- 🏠 **Applications**: Smart home automation, energy-saving systems, and privacy-preserving occupancy monitoring.
- ⚙️ **Advantages**: Low-cost, camera-free, and effective in enclosed indoor environments.

> This project presents a novel approach to passive human sensing without relying on visual data or wearable devices.
